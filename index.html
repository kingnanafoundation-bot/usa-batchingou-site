Sons & Daughters of Batchingou · United States

One village, fifty states, one family.

The USA Batchingou Community connects the American diaspora of the Batchingou chiefdom — Ndé, West Region, Cameroon — through mutual aid, culture, and a shared commitment to home. Wherever you've settled, there's a place for you here.

What we do

A home away from home, organized like one

Four pillars carry the work of the community, echoed in the four points of the Ngong that borders this page.

01

Solidarity

Emergency support for members and families facing hardship, bereavement, or medical need — wherever they live in the US.

02

Culture

Keeping the Kougang dance, language, and chieftaincy traditions alive for children raised far from the village.

03

Development

Funding school, health, and infrastructure projects back home in Batchingou, in partnership with village elders.

04

Belonging

State chapters, an annual assembly, and a calendar of gatherings so no member feels alone in the diaspora.
